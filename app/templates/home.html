The History of Exalted

Do not believe what the scientists tell you. The natural history we know is a lie, a falsehood sold to us by wicked old men who would make the world a dull gray prison and protect us from the dangers inherent to freedom. They would have you believe our planet to be a lonely starship, hurtling through the void of space, barren of magic and in need of a stern hand upon the rudder.

Close your eyes to their deception. The time before our time was not a time of senseless natural struggle and reptilian rage, but a time of myth and sorcery. It was a time of legend, when heroes walked Creation and wielded the very power of the Gods. It was a time before the world was bent, a time before the magic of Creation lessened, a time before the souls of men became the stunted, withered things they are today.

This is the story of that time.
This is the story of the Exalted.

Primordial Age

Before Creation was, the Primordials were. Each Primordial was a titan of colossal strength composed by many other creatures of insane might. Think of how a man is composed of cells, then the Primordials were composed of Gods. These creatures, so tall and mighty that the human cannot fully comprehend them, gathered together, and forged Creation out of their own will.

And at the very top of Creation, they forged the Game of Divinity, and there they made merry for eons uncountable. Such was the joy of the game, that they never ceased playing. Creation decayed around them, so the Primordials proposed to forge lesser beings, servants to care for Creation while they played the game.

Thus were the Gods made.
Thus did the Primordials doom themselves.

The Primordial War

Mightiest among the Gods was the Unconquered Sun. For the wicked dragon, the ever-failing primordial that embodied all that was wicked, decided to forge a god in direct opposition to itself, and so was the ever-victorious Unconquered Sun made, and nothing was beyond his grasp. Alas, he himself was bound as the Gods were, and could do naught against the Primordials who acted as they willed.

But one of the Primordials, the Father of Man, betrayed his fellows, and composed a great plan. "Give of me thine lifeblood, and with it I shall forge mighty blades, and mine own children shall wield it, and the Primordials will flee in dread." So it was that some of the highest Gods gave of their own essence, and Exaltations were forged.

So it was that war began between mankind and the Primordials, as the Gods acted in the shadows, doing all they could to sabotage their masters. The war raged on for many years, as the Primordials saw it as nothing more than entertainment, but one day everything changed.

Because to be a Solar is to reflect the great, triumphant, tyrannical light of the sun. It's to know that, no matter what challenge arises, it can be overcome. So it was that, after so long fighting against the immortal Primordials, the first Solars achieved the impossible, and slew what could not be slain, for no challenge is beyond the grasp of the Unconquered Sun.

And that which cannot die, died. And reality wept at the sight, and the impossible became possible, and the Primordials feared. Three more were slain before the Primordials surrendered. For that, they earned eternal imprisonment, and jailed they remain, forever raging against the rebellion of man.

Of the dead rotting Primordials, who cannot die and yet are dead, we best not speak of. For there lies a path to madness.

Thus with an impossible deed, Creation earned its freedom.
Thus began the glorious First Age.

The First Age

Upon the very top of Creation, the Unconquered Sun, and his virtue was without end, and his wisdom was beyond all. Through his children, the men who he gave his lifeblood to, he ruled Creation. So it was that men were ruled by wise Solar-King, men who could do the impossible, if they simply tried hard enough.

No age since can be compared to it. Every wonder that is, can be sourced to this age, when Solar-craftsmen were free to work as they willed, and sought to create more and more glorious things as to please their father. Alas, alas, all that is good much come to an end, and of this age, we know nothing more than that, for its end was most foul.

Mankind once more rose in rebellion, this time against their virtuous kings, calling them harsh tyrants and worse. Beguiled by wicked Gods and greedy men, they made war against their rulers, and through trickery, slew most of them.

And, behold, the Unconquered Sun watched his children perish, and he turned his face away from Creation.
And, behold, the Age of Bronze began.

The Age of Bronze

The inheritors of Creation were nowhere good as its previous leaders, and many wars followed their rebellion, and many great foes rose against them. The dead no longer died, but gnawed on the dead bodies of the immortal Primordials in the Underworld, and there they grew mighty, and made war upon the living. And the caged Primordials rattled against their cages, sending forth legions of evil upon the world.

And the servants who has slain the Solars made war against each other, and the impossible wonders crafted by those so beloved by the Unconquered Sun vanished from the world. And that which lies beyond Creation, the primordial Chaos of infinite potential, also spewed into that which is made, creating impossible creatures that destroyed much.

This chaos seemed without end, until, at last, a young woman stumbled into a great weapon of the Solars, and with this weapon, she smote Creation, and slew her foes by the thousands, destroying whole nations on a whim. She abandoned her name, and became the Scarlet Empress, and has ruled Creation since. Her descendants are legion, mighty and powerful like no mortal can be, and she has crafted a faith of her own, and has spread it to the world, making all believe that she stands upon the pinnacle of mankind.

And she was wise and cunning and wicked, and so she made sure to empower many people who hated each other, making it so that they could never work togehter without her. Everyone knew that her death would herald the worst civil war to ever mar Creation, which meant that, no matter how much some hated her, they could not slay her. It was an excellent plan.

It has been six months since she vanished.
She will not return...

The Return of the Solars

The Realm that rules the world is coming apart at the seams. With every passing day, the legions grow more convinced that the Scarlet Empress has not simply taken a vacation, but has truly vanished. Her children already wage genocidal and fraternal wars against each other. Whole kingdoms of descedants poisoning and slaying each other.

And the dead crawl out of their graves, led by Death-Lords and Abyssals, and the Fae march upon Creation again, and fell Gods awaken from beneath the world, and the Primordials seek to escape their cage yet again. It's a time of chaos. Of death. Creation has stood against similar threats before, but never all of them at once. If men knew of the coming suffering of the ages, they would surely despair.

But there is yet hope.

“You who have suffered in bondage are now free. I am the Unconquered Sun, and I have seen your struggles. I hear the voices of all hearts that cry out for justice and righteousness in this world. Once, I turned my face away and made myself deaf to those cries, but no longer. As you raise your hand and your voice in the cause of righteousness, my son, I Exalt you above all others. Go, and bring your light into the darkness. Make right the world.”

For the Unconquered Sun has turned his eyes back upon Creation, and has bled over man once more, and Solars walk the earth again.

They shall be hated by all men, for generation have lived and died knowing only the wicked tales spread by the servants who slew them. They shall face threats in every way, in every direction. The Gods will be their foes, as will be the dead, and the fae, and the Primordials. So rise, son of the Sun, rise and conquer it all.

...and try to not fall to madness, as your predecessors did. For such power is the most wicked of poisons.
